## Limits

These are qualitative agent-behaviour evaluations, not deterministic proof. The
corpus unit test checks case structure and skill presence only. Change to the
skill, protected cases, planning rules, runner, model, or reasoning settings
requires the affected fresh-context evaluations to be rerun.
